# Gravewright

**Your world. Your players. Your next great session.**

[English](https://github.com/Gravewright/.github/blob/main/profile/README.md) · [Português (Brasil)](https://github.com/Gravewright/.github/blob/main/profile/README.pt-BR.md)

Gravewright is a free, open-source virtual tabletop for your RPG campaigns. Prepare
the scene, gather your group in the browser and keep maps, characters and session
tools together in a world you host and control.

**[Explore Gravewright](https://gravewright.com)** · [Download](https://github.com/Gravewright/gravewright/releases/latest) · [Getting started](https://github.com/Gravewright/gravewright/blob/main/docs/en/getting-started.md)

### A place for your campaign

- **Set the atmosphere.** Build scenes with lighting, fog, walls, audio and visual effects. Reveal the world at your pace.
- **Bring your characters.** Use PDF sheets, map their fields and connect characters to tokens on the board.
- **Keep the session moving.** Roll dice, share notes, draw cards and follow combat without leaving the table.
- **Carry the story forward.** Keep journals, quests, handouts and discoveries together between sessions.
- **Make the table yours.** Host your own installation and extend it with systems and modules from the Marketplace.

### Start your first table

Download the complete project ZIP, extract it and open **Install Windows.bat**.
The installer prepares your installation and creates **Gravewright Runner.bat**.
Open the Runner, create your administrator account in the browser and start your
campaign. Windows 10 (1803+) or Windows 11, x64, is required for this launcher.

The Runner starts locally on your computer. To invite players over your network
or the internet, follow the [hosting guide](https://github.com/Gravewright/gravewright/blob/main/docs/en/deployment.md).
Campaign data is stored separately from the application; the
[Runner guide](https://github.com/Gravewright/gravewright/blob/main/docs/en/windows-runner.md)
explains settings and backups. Other hosting options are covered in the documentation.

### Available now: 0.2.0 Alpha

Version 0.2.0 Alpha brings independent system sheets, PDF System combat with turn
indicators, permission-aware campaign search, GM support layers and a more
consistent interface. Module authors also gain private translation catalogs
through the VTT API, documented in Translator 0.1.2.

**[Download Gravewright 0.2.0 Alpha](https://github.com/Gravewright/gravewright/releases/tag/v0.2.0).**
Gravewright remains in active Alpha development; keep backups of
important campaigns and check the [user guide](https://github.com/Gravewright/gravewright/blob/main/docs/en/user-guide.md)
for supported workflows.
